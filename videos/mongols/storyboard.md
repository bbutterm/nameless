# Who Ruled the 1200s? The Mongols — storyboard

**One idea:** a tiny people on an ocean of grass takes the crown of the world in 80 years — and the reasons are things anyone can understand: speed, organization, information, learning, fear.

**Series thread (all "Who Ruled the World" episodes):** a globe + a golden **crown** that sits on the century's leading power, and the **century ribbon** (1200 ——●—— 1300) at the top. Each episode begins with the crown where the last one left it and ends with it lifting off toward the next century.

**Episode thread:** **horses.** One grazing horse opens the video; the boy's nine horses (eight stolen); riders switching horses; the Yam relay; the closing joke ("…lost eight of its nine horses") over a horizon full of horses.

**How it differs from earlier videos** (Basilisk: indigo night, eye, badges 1/2/3, dark synth; Paperclips: desk + counter, factory):
- World: daylight steppe + globe/maps, warm gold grass and the "Eternal Blue Sky". No eye, no aiCore.
- Palette: steppe gold `#e2b04a`, sky blue `#4aa3d8 → #bfe6f5`, Mongol blue `#2f5fd0`, felt white `#f3ead8`, war red `#d94a3a`; dark "fear" act in rust/charcoal. Night indigo only in the plague scene.
- Reasons device: a **quiver of five arrows** (each reason pulls an arrow), not number badges.
- Music: steppe drone (open fifths, overtone "throat" drone), bowed-string melody (morin-khuur-like), frame drum and **gallop rhythm in 6/8**; darker low drum for "fear", calm pads for "Pax Mongolica", night drone for the plague.
- Transitions: globe ↔ landscape dives (camera falls through clouds), map wipes along routes, match cut horse ↔ map rider token.
